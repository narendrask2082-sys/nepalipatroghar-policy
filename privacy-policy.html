Privacy Policy

Nepali Patro Ghar - Calendar (नेपाली पात्रो घर)

Effective Date: October 7, 2026
Last Updated: October 7, 2026

Welcome to Nepali Patro Ghar. We respect the privacy of our users and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding information collection, usage, and permissions.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Nepali Patro Ghar is built on an offline-first architecture:

  • We do not collect or store personal identifiable information (such as your name, email, phone number, or physical address).
  • We do not track your precise GPS coordinates.
  • All personalized features (Personal Notes, Event Reminders, and Period Tracker logs) are stored strictly locally on your device. No sensitive data is transmitted to external servers.

2. Permissions Requested

The app requests minimum permissions strictly required for core functionality:

  • Notification Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to deliver daily calendar notifications, Panchanga alerts, user-configured reminders, and festival greetings.
  • Boot Completion (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used solely to re-register daily alarms and refresh home screen widgets when the device restarts.
  • Internet & Network State: Used solely to fetch publicly available updated calendar data and holiday announcements.

3. Children’s Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The calendar content is suitable and safe for all age groups.

4. Third-Party Services

The application does not include intrusive data mining, surveillance, or personal profiling trackers.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
