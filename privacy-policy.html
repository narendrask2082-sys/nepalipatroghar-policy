Privacy Policy — Mega Patro (मेगा पात्रो)

Effective Date: November 25, 2026  |  Developer: Mega Info Tech, Nepal (Narendra SK)  |  Package: com.megapatronepali.calendar

1. Overview

Mega Patro ("the App") is a Nepali Calendar, Panchanga, Daily Notification Widget, and Period Tracker application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

Summary: We do not collect, store, or share any personal data on external servers. All personal preferences and Period Tracker data remain strictly on your device.

2. Information We Collect

  • No Account Required: You can use all features of Mega Patro without creating an account or providing personal identifiers.
  • Local Storage Only: Your app preferences, theme settings, language selection, and Period Tracker entries are stored exclusively on your device using Android local storage (SharedPreferences).
  • Calendar Data Sync: The App fetches public, non-personal Nepali calendar and rashifal JSON data from GitHub to keep festival and calendar information up to date. No user data is sent during this sync.

3. Health & Period Tracker Data

Mega Patro includes an optional Period Tracker feature:

  • All cycle dates, notes, and reminder preferences are saved strictly on your device.
  • This health-related data is never transmitted to any server, never sold, and never shared with any third party.
  • Notification reminders use generic wording to protect your privacy on the lock screen.
  • You can permanently delete all locally stored period data at any time by clearing the App's data in Android Settings or uninstalling the App.

4. Permissions Used

  • Internet (INTERNET) & Network State (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used only to fetch public calendar/panchanga updates and open external links when you tap them.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to display the optional daily Nepali date notification bar widget and local reminders.
  • Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used to restore your home screen widgets and daily date notification bar after restarting your phone.

5. Third-Party Services & Links

The App does not include third-party advertising SDKs or analytics trackers. If you tap external contact links (such as WhatsApp or Facebook) in the About section, you will be directed to those external services, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Mega Patro does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
